Genesis player support

Afterlight: Genesis is the Sector 07 survival strategy game.

Recover your progress

In Settings, open Save & recovery to export a local copy. If you uploaded a civilization backup, sign in to the same account under Cloud backup & account to restore it. Restoring replaces your current civilization on this device.

Report a game issue

Include your device, the screen you were on and what happened. Reports are public. Leave out passwords, account email addresses and save files.

Game issue tracker

Privacy

The game stores local progress, preferences and signed-in session information on your device. You can play the local civilization without creating an account. Clearing app or site data may remove your offline progress.

When you sign up, the configured Supabase service processes your email and password for authentication. Cloud backups store the civilization you upload. Online play stores information such as your base, callsign, actions, rewards, rankings, alliance membership and chat. Other players can see information displayed in the shared world, rankings and alliance features.

GitHub Pages serves the web game. Some artwork and 3D libraries load from fal.media and jsDelivr. These services receive network requests, including your IP address, when their content loads.

Request account deletion

Open the game, choose Settings → Cloud backup & account, and sign in with the account you want removed. Choose Request account deletion and confirm. This sends a private request for removal of your account, cloud backup and associated game data. Your offline copy stays on your device. The web game supports this request even if you have uninstalled the app.
