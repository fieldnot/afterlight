# Afterlight: Genesis

## Subtitle
Rebuild Sector 07

## Short description
Rebuild your bunker, lead your squad and decide who gets through the blast door.

## Store description
Someone is knocking at the blast door. Your bunker has power, a handful of survivors and an AI waiting for your orders. Your first choice is whether to let the strangers in.

Build Sector 07 room by room. Grow food, restore power and upgrade the AI Core to open new operations. Recruit commanders, outfit them in the armory and take a squad into the ruins. Read the enemy formation, choose your counter and time EVA’s Overdrive to turn a fight.

The solo civilization saves on your device. An optional online account adds cloud backups and access to the shared Genesis map, where squads bring supplies back from mines.
